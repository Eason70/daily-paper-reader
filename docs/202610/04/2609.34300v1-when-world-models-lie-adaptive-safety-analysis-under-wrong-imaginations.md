## 摘要
世界模型为高维机器人系统中的安全推理提供了强大的基础，但它们也并非可靠：其预测可能有偏、校准不当，或自信地给出错误预测。这给潜在空间安全滤波器带来了核心挑战，这类滤波器通常基于世界模型的动力学学习 Hamilton-Jacobi 安全价值函数。如果世界模型不正确，所得价值函数会继承其误差，并产生过度自信的安全估计。现有潜在安全滤波器通常依赖集成分歧或价值目标一致性残差等辅助信号来进行自适应，但即使世界模型的预测偏离观测，这些信号也可能仍然很小。我们提出一种自适应潜在安全滤波器，利用直接观测到的世界模型误差来校准安全推理。我们的方法使用自适应保形推断（Adaptive Conformal Inference）根据预测潜在状态与观测推断潜在状态之间的差异构建在线不确定性集，然后通过在这些集合上最小化学习到的价值函数来悲观地评估安全性。这使滤波器在世界模型准确时保持最小程度的保守，而在观测揭示模型不匹配时变得更加谨慎。我们为自适应不确定性半径提供了有限时间覆盖保证。通过仿真和硬件实验，我们表明，与最先进的潜在安全滤波器相比，我们的方法在保持任务完成率的同时显著减少了失败。

## Abstract
World models offer a powerful substrate for safety reasoning in high-dimensional robotic systems, but they are also fallible: their predictions can be biased, miscalibrated, or confidently wrong. This creates a central challenge for latent-space safety filters, which often learn Hamilton-Jacobi safety value functions on the dynamics of a world model. If the world model is incorrect, the resulting value function can inherit its errors and produce overconfident safety estimates. Existing latent safety filters often rely on auxiliary signals such as ensemble disagreement or value-target consistency residuals for adaptation, but these signals can remain small even when the world model's predictions deviate from observations. We propose an adaptive latent safety filter that calibrates safety reasoning using directly observed world-model error. Our method uses Adaptive Conformal Inference to construct online uncertainty sets from discrepancies between predicted and observation-inferred latent states, then evaluates safety pessimistically by minimizing the learned value function over these sets. This allows the filter to remain minimally conservative when the world model is accurate, while becoming more cautious when observations reveal model mismatch. We provide a finite-time coverage guarantee for the adaptive uncertainty radius. Through simulation and hardware experiments, we show that our method significantly reduces failures relative to state-of-the-art latent safety filters while preserving task completion.

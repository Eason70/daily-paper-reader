## 摘要
控制屏障函数是一种有效的基于模型的工具，可用于形式化地认证系统的安全性。然而，将其理论保证迁移到真实世界的机器人系统需要高模型保真度。例如，有效载荷或风扰会对飞行器造成显著的模型扰动，从而导致安全性受损。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种可认证的在线学习增强鲁棒自适应控制屏障函数，它使用神经常微分方程（Neural ODE）适应扰动，并通过共形预测量化其自适应不确定性。我们的方法在未知时变模型扰动下始终保证安全性。当自适应不确定性较高时，它采用保守策略；随着获得更多数据，它高效地进行自适应以降低控制器的保守性。在底层模型和轨迹满足适当的 Lipschitz 光滑性假设下，我们的方法提供了具有概率界的可证明安全性保证。这些结果证明了我们的方法作为一种实用安全控制器，在模型扰动下运行的机器人系统中的潜力。

## Abstract
Control barrier functions are an effective model-based tool to formally certify the safety of a system. However, transferring their theoretical guarantees to real-world robotics systems requires high model fidelity. For example, payloads or wind disturbances can cause significant model perturbations to an aerial vehicle, leading to safety compromises. In this work, we propose a certifiable online learning-enhanced robust adaptive control barrier function, which adapts to disturbances using a Neural ODE and quantifies its adaptation uncertainty with conformal prediction. Our approach guarantees safety at all time under unknown time-varying model disturbances. It adopts a conservative strategy when the adaptation uncertainty is high; and efficiently adapts to reduce controller conservativeness as it receives more data. Our approach provides a provable safety guarantee with a probability bound under suitable Lipschitz smoothness assumptions on the underlying model and trajectory. These results demonstrate the potential of our method as a practical safety controller for robotics system operating under model perturbations.

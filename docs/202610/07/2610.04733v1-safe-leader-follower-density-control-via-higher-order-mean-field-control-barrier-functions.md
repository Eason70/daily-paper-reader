## 摘要
大规模多智能体系统的安全滤波器可以在种群层面通过平均场控制障碍函数（MF-CBF）来构建。大多数现有表述都是一阶的，要求控制输入显式出现在安全泛函的一阶时间导数中；高阶扩展直到最近才针对单个、直接驱动的种群被提出。我们则考虑如下情形：需要保持安全的种群并非直接驱动，而是由第二个受控种群生成的场输运。针对这一设定，我们为密度动力学开发了一个高阶 MF-CBF 框架，其中安全约束的相对阶大于一。我们的主要结果是，这种间接驱动不会破坏安全条件的控制仿射结构：安全泛函二阶时间导数中依赖于控制的部分可闭式得到，并且关于控制速度场是线性的，其核由受驱动密度乘以一阶变分的空间梯度给出。因此，相对阶为二的安全约束仍可通过凸二次规划来强制执行。随后，我们将该框架应用于存在危险区域的领导者-跟随者密度控制问题，其中安全性通过使每个种群与这些区域的重叠低于规定阈值来保证。我们表明，跟随者安全泛函相对于领导者控制场具有相对阶二，而领导者安全泛函具有相对阶一。我们进一步精确刻画了两个同时约束不可行的条件，引入自适应增益机制以缓解潜在冲突，并给出了安全校正所诱导的跟踪误差界。在杂乱环境中的数值仿真说明了由此产生的安全-性能权衡。

## Abstract
Safety filters for large-scale multi-agent systems can be formulated at the population level through mean-field control barrier functions (MF-CBFs). Most existing formulations are first-order, requiring the control input to appear explicitly in the first time derivative of the safety functional; higher-order extensions have only very recently been proposed for a single, directly actuated population. We consider instead the case in which the population to be kept safe is not directly actuated, but is transported by a field generated by a second, controlled population. For this setting, we develop a higher-order MF-CBF framework for density dynamics with safety constraints of relative degree larger than one. Our main result is that indirect actuation of this kind does not destroy the control-affine structure of the safety condition: the control-dependent part of the second time derivative of the safety functional is obtained in closed form and is linear in the control velocity field, with a kernel given by the actuated density times the spatial gradient of a first variation. Relative-degree-two safety constraints can therefore still be enforced through a convex quadratic program. We then specialize the framework to a leader-follower density control problem in the presence of dangerous regions, where safety is ensured by keeping the overlap of each population with those regions below a prescribed threshold. We show that the follower safety functional has relative degree two with respect to the leader control field, while the leader safety functional has relative degree one. We further characterize exactly when the two simultaneous constraints are infeasible, introduce an adaptive-gain mechanism to mitigate potential conflicts, and bound the tracking errors induced by the safety correction. Numerical simulations in cluttered environments illustrate the resulting safety-performance trade-off.

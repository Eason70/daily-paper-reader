## 摘要
自主社交导航需要在效率、物理安全和社会规范遵从之间取得平衡。强化学习（RL）方法提供了可行且有效的解决方案，但往往依赖于不切实际的假设，例如已知人类的位置和速度。本文提出了 JESSI（基于 JAX 的端到端安全社交可解释导航），一个轻量级端到端 RL 框架，将原始 LiDAR 扫描直接映射为运动学可行的控制指令。JESSI 通过 Dirichlet 参数化的连续动作空间和确定性边界来增强安全性，同时集成的基于注意力的感知模块提取概率化的人类状态，以实现可解释、具有社交意识的决策。通过大量仿真以及在差速驱动机器人上的真实世界部署，我们证明，在多任务范式下联合优化 RL 策略与监督感知信号能够增强社交行为。最终，相较于最先进的基线方法，JESSI 能够兼顾高导航成功率和更优的社交行为。

## Abstract
Autonomous social navigation requires balancing efficiency, physical safety, and social compliance. Reinforcement Learning (RL) methods provide a viable and effective solution but often rely on unrealistic assumptions, such as the knowledge of humans' position and velocity. In this paper, we introduce JESSI (JAX-based E2E Safe Social Interpretable navigation), a lightweight end-to-end RL framework that maps raw LiDAR scans directly to kinematically feasible control commands. JESSI enhances safety via Dirichlet-parameterized continuous action spaces and deterministic bounding, while an integrated attention-based perception module extracts probabilistic human states for interpretable, socially aware decision-making. Through extensive simulations and real-world deployment on a differential-drive robot, we demonstrate that jointly optimizing the RL policy with a supervised perception signal in a multi-task paradigm enhances social behavior. Ultimately, JESSI is able to balance high navigation success rates and superior social behaviors compared to state-of-the-art baselines.
